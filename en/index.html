Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the app “MtG Wishlist” (the “App”). This English page is a translation of the Japanese privacy policy. If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Data we collect

1. Anonymous price data (collected automatically)

When you mark a card as purchased, and when you view a card, the App sends the following data anonymously to the developer’s server (Supabase). When you view a card, only the card printing ID and the finish are sent, and no price is sent (this registers the card for recording its price history).

ItemDetails
Card printing IDScryfall’s card printing identifier (not personal information)
Finishfoil / nonfoil / etched
Target price (JPY)The target purchase price you entered
ConditionOnly if set (NM / EX / GD)
Format tagOnly if set (Standard / Modern, etc.)

Data we do not send: The App does not send a device ID, Apple ID, email address, location or any other personal information. No identifier is attached to any submission, so it is technically impossible to link multiple submissions to the same user.

2. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information such as name, address or contact details
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs, etc.)
  • Location
  • The contents of your wishlist (card names, quantities, notes, etc.)
  • Browsing or search history

How we use the data

The price data we collect is used only for statistical aggregation: to calculate the reference prices shown in the App (actual prices paid in Japan) and to record each card’s price history. It is not used to identify individuals, for advertising, for sale to third parties or for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Price samples are excluded from aggregation after 90 days from collection. Individual samples are deleted periodically after aggregation. Each card’s price history (daily aggregated values that cannot be linked to any individual submission or user) is retained.

Sharing with third parties

We do not provide collected data to advertisers, data brokers or any other third parties. Server-side aggregation is performed only by the developer. The App uses Supabase as its server infrastructure, and Supabase’s privacy policy applies.

The App also uses the third-party Scryfall API to search cards and to fetch card data and images. These requests (including search terms and card identifiers) are sent directly to Scryfall, and Scryfall’s privacy policy applies. The developer does not receive the contents of these requests.
