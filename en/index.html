Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the app “MtG Wishlist” (the “App”). This English page is a translation of the Japanese privacy policy. If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Data we collect

1. Anonymous price data (collected automatically)

When you mark a card as purchased, and when you view a card, the App sends the following data anonymously to the developer’s server. When you view a card, only the card printing ID and the finish are sent, and no price is sent (this registers the card for recording its price history). To prioritize updating the market prices of cards that many people add, the App also sends the printing and finish of each card you add and the App version. No identifier is included. To keep updating the market prices of the cards currently on your list, once a month the App sends the printing and finish of those cards.

ItemDetails
Card printing IDScryfall’s card printing identifier (not personal information)
Finishfoil / nonfoil / etched
Target price (JPY)The target purchase price you entered
ConditionOnly if set (NM / EX / GD)
Format tagOnly if set (Standard / Modern, etc.)

Data we do not send: The App does not send a device ID, Apple ID, email address, location or any other personal information. No identifier is attached to any submission, so it is technically impossible to link multiple submissions to the same user.

2. Usage milestones (collected automatically)

To understand how far people get with the App’s features, the App sends the name of a “milestone” and the app version anonymously to the developer’s server the first time you reach it. Each milestone is sent only once per device.

Example milestones
Opened the App; opened it again on a later day / 7 or more days later / 30 or more days later
Added a card; reached each wishlist size from 1 to 10 cards; entered a target price; set a price-drop alert; recorded a purchase
Reached the free limit; viewed the Pro screen; tapped the purchase button; became Pro (for the Pro screen, also counted by where it was opened from: the free limit, deck import, purchase history or a widget)
Used deck import, saving a share image, the token display, purchase history or saving a backup
Switched the list to List view; opened it in List view on a later day; added a Home Screen widget
Opened the long-press action circle; deleted a card; deleted cards from each place: the long-press circle, swiping a List row, deleting everything, or deleting a format
Opened the App by tapping a price alert or a new set notification

Only the milestone name and the app version are sent. No identifier, card, price or wishlist content is sent. It is technically impossible to tell which device a milestone came from or to link one device’s milestones together.

To know how many devices use the App each day and how long people keep using it, the App also sends, anonymously and once on each day you open it, how long ago you started using the App (in ranges: the first day, day 1, days 2–6, days 7–13, days 14–29, day 30 or later) and the size of your wishlist (in ranges: 0, 1–4, 5 or more). The server keeps only a count per date and range; it does not record which device a count came from.

In the same way, to know how many devices use the App each week and each month, the App sends, anonymously and once in each week and once in each month you open it, whether you started using the App in that week or month. The server keeps only a count per week or month and group; it does not record which device a count came from.

To know what share of devices keep using the App after they start, the App sends, anonymously and once each, the week you started using the App and the period, on the day you start and the first time you open the App in each later period: day 1, days 2–6, days 7–13, days 14–29, days 30–59, and day 60 or later. The server keeps only a count per week and period; it does not record which device a count came from.

Milestones and these usage counts also include whether you use the Japanese or the overseas edition, that is, whether the App is set to Japanese. They are sent in every language.

3. Daily close notifications

If you allow notifications, or while the App delivers notifications quietly to Notification Center before asking for your permission, the App sends the following data to the developer’s server so that it can tell you every night at 9 p.m. in your device’s time zone how the prices on your wishlist moved. This applies in every language; outside Japan the notification uses overseas prices.

ItemDetails
Notification addressThe push notification address that Apple issues for each device and app
Wishlist cardsOnly the card printing ID, finish and the number of copies you still need
Language and editionJapanese or English, Japanese or overseas edition
CurrencyYen, dollars or euros
Time zoneYour device’s time zone, such as Europe/Paris
App versionThe version of the App that sent the data

Card names, notes, wish prices, purchase records, your Apple ID and device identifiers are not sent. Each night the server writes the notification from the prices of these cards and sends it through Apple’s push notification service. Nothing is sent on days when none of your cards moved.

If you stop the daily close notification in the App, the data is deleted right away. If you turn off notifications in iOS Settings, choose Turn Off on a notification delivered quietly to Notification Center, or your wishlist becomes empty, the data is deleted the next time you open the App. If you delete the App, the data is deleted as soon as Apple reports that the address is no longer valid.

4. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information such as name, address or contact details
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs, etc.)
  • Location
  • The contents of your wishlist (card names, quantities, notes, etc.; except the wishlist size ranges sent with the milestones and the daily usage count above, and the cards held for the daily close notification)
  • Browsing or search history (the usage milestones above only record whether a feature was used for the first time, not what you viewed or searched for)

How we use the data

The price data we collect is used only for statistical aggregation: to calculate the reference prices shown in the App (actual prices paid in Japan), to record each card’s price history, and to count how often each card is added. Usage milestones, daily, weekly and monthly usage counts and continued-use counts are used only for counting, to improve the App. The data for the daily close notification is used only to write and send that notification. None of it is used to identify individuals, for advertising, for sale to third parties or for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Price samples are excluded from aggregation after 90 days from collection. Individual samples are deleted periodically after aggregation. Each card’s price history (daily aggregated values that cannot be linked to any individual submission or user) is retained. Usage milestones (only the milestone name, the app version and the time received), daily, weekly and monthly usage counts (only a count per date, week or month and range) and continued-use counts (only a count per week and period) are retained for counting. The notification address and cards held for the daily close notification are deleted when you turn notifications off, empty your wishlist or delete the App. Each notification text is deleted 30 days after it is sent.

Sharing with third parties

We do not provide collected data to advertisers, data brokers or any other third parties. Server-side aggregation is performed only by the developer. The App’s server runs on a third-party cloud service.

The App also uses the third-party Scryfall API to search cards and to fetch card data and images. These requests (including search terms and card identifiers) are sent directly to Scryfall, and Scryfall’s privacy policy applies. The developer does not receive the contents of these requests.
